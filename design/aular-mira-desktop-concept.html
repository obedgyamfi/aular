Product principles

AULAR should make delegated work inspectable without forcing users to supervise every step. Agents act autonomously inside clear boundaries and surface decisions only when product intent or external consequences require a person.

  • Show work, ownership, progress, and blockers.
  • Preserve context across conversations, tasks, and routines.
  • Require approval for consequential external actions.
