Product principles

AULAR is an organization, not a chatbot. The interface must make delegation, ownership, progress, evidence, and user intervention visible without turning every agent action into noise.

  • Keep the conversation and the work behind it connected.
  • Surface decisions at the point where human intent is required.
  • Never claim a tool succeeded without evidence.
  • Keep data local unless the user explicitly moves it.
